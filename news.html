Colossal Biosciences has completed its first acquisition, bringing Austin-based Viagen Pets & Equine into the company to accelerate species-preservation efforts. Viagen adds two decades of expertise in assisted reproduction, cryopreservation, and biobanking—capabilities that have supported work with threatened and endangered animals.
