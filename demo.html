STEP 01

Ancient DNA
Recovery

Description

Scientists begin by locating exceptionally preserved remains such as bones, teeth, or hair from extinct animals. These samples provide the highest chance of recovering usable ancient DNA.

Q&A
How can DNA survive for thousands of years?
Cold, dry and stable environments help preserve fragments of DNA long after an animal becomes extinct.
STEP 02

DNA
Extraction

Description

Inside contamination-controlled laboratories, researchers isolate tiny fragments of ancient DNA, preparing them for sequencing and genome analysis.

Q&A
Why are clean laboratories essential?
Ancient DNA is extremely fragile. Even tiny amounts of modern DNA contamination can affect the results.
