In film, thylacines are often cast as something to fear. In games, they’re reimagined as heroes, shifting the story from threat to identity.

Ty the
Tasmanian Tiger

Ty the Tasmanian Tiger is a platforming game where the main character is, you guessed it a Tasmanian Tiger. Your trusty boomerang and special abilities take you throughout environments in Australia as you battle to get your friends and family back from a supernatural realm. (Thylacines are not known to have worn shorts or been able to talk.)

[ INDEX ]+

Thylacines don’t typically appear in the official versions of these games, but they show up through community-created mods. Players have added them in as realistic zoo animals, focusing on conservation and ecological accuracy rather than exaggeration. The fact that these versions are fan-made says a lot. People want the thylacine in these worlds, even when the games don’t include it.

ARK:SURVIVAL EVOLVED

Thylacines don’t show up in the game, but the Hyaenodon kind of fills their spot. It’s a smaller, pack-hunting carnivore built more for tracking and stamina than brute force, which lines up pretty well with how thylacines likely lived.
