With five labs and 17 partner labs, Colossal is still driven by the same mission: ending extinction and solving the planet’s biggest challenges. Today, the company has successfully revived the dire wolf and is developing world’s firsts in core reproductive and gene-editing platforms, leading to key milestones across mammoth, thylacine, dodo, and the moa de-extinction projects. We’ve even found ourselves getting attention from across the media spectrum, with coverage in CNN, The New York Times, Vox, Fox News, and The Wall Street Journal.
