Explore how the mammoth shaped culture and inspired lore

For many people, their first encounter with the mammoth came not through paleontology, but through popular culture. Mammoths loom large across entertainment—appearing in fantasy role-playing games like Dungeons & Dragons, survival worlds such as ARK: Survival Evolved, and cinematic spectacles that reimagined the Ice Age for modern audiences. Most memorably, the animated film Ice Age introduced millions to Manny, a gentle giant whose strength, loyalty, and emotional depth helped define the mammoth as more than a prehistoric relic—transforming it into a cultural icon that bridges ancient life and modern imagination.
