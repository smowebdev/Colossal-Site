Biodiversity is critical to the health and stability of our planet, supporting ecosystems, food systems, and human well-being. The biodiversity crisis is escalating, and it’s our moment to rise to the challenge. With science as our tool, we can protect and rebuild the living systems that power our planet.

Biodiversity is fundamental to food security

More than 25% of non-domesticated, livestock-related species and more than 15% of animal pollinator species are at risk of extinction; the world relies on 3 crops to feed the world.

Biodiversity stamps out disease reservoirs helping to avoid the next pandemic

More than 75% of new diseases come from the erosion of human-wild interface (AIDS, Ebola, H1N1, and COVID-19) and a diverse biodiversity set helps to regulate species disease versus human interference, which leads to zoonotic diseases.

WORK IN ACTION

COLOSSAL'S CONSERVATION WORK EXTENDS FAR BEYOND DE-EXTINCTION.

The extinction crisis isn’t slowing down, and neither can we. At Colossal, we’re developing conservation technologies that help species adapt and recover in a rapidly changing world. Restoring lost diversity to engineering resilience against emerging threats like climate change and diseases, we're committed to rectifying past harms and rehabilitating nature on a global scale. Because the future of conservation depends on solutions that move as fast as the challenges.
